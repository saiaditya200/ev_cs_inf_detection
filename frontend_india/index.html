Methodological Architecture & Three Tracks

National Policy Alignment
Track A

Observed Station-Level Classification

Learns DC Fast infrastructure suitability (>= 50kW vs AC Slow) across 52,718 public charging stations using 21 non-leaky spatial, road, and demographic features. Evaluated with 5-fold state-grouped spatial cross-validation.

Track B

Macro-Level State EV Adoption Context

Ingests official Ministry of Heavy Industries & VAHAN national statistics across all 36 States/UTs. Quantifies EV penetration rate per 1,000 vehicles without circular station-level demand proxy.

Track C

National Grid Capacity & Nodal Agency Context

Incorporates state nodal agency frameworks (BESCOM, MSEDCL, UPNEDA, DTL, TEDA, TGREDCO) and high-capacity transmission corridor connectivity along national expressways.
